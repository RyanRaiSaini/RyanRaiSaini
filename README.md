## Projects

- [Empyrean](https://github.com/richardlancs/Empyrean): AI-powered smart glasses for blind and low-vision people, built by our team of four in 36 hours at HackUMass XIV. A camera on the glasses tracks the wearer's fingertips, and when they touch something, a speaker says exactly what it is, down to the brand ("Heinz ketchup", not "bottle"). The glasses also sound an alarm when the wearer walks toward stairs, a drop or a vehicle, and keep a touch history so you can ask "where did I last touch the ketchup?". We built a 10-module real-time pipeline in Python (YOLO11, MediaPipe, OpenCV, Grok Vision, FastAPI, Tiger Data), fine-tuned YOLO11 on 8 custom objects to 0.885 mAP50, and named brands correctly 54 out of 60 times in a median of 435 ms. It has offline voices, a live dashboard and 330+ automated tests, and we pitched it to the SpaceX and Gemini track judges.
